New to this? How to read the page
Projection
The model's best guess for a player's stat in tonight's game, for example 25 points.
DraftKings line
The number the sportsbook sets for that stat. A bet is on whether the player goes Over or stays Under it. ▲ means the model's guess is above the line, ▼ below.
Odds (−110, +150)
The price of a bet. −110 means you risk $110 to win $100. +150 means you risk $100 to win $150. Hover over any odds to see this. The sportsbook builds in a fee, so a pick usually has to win 52–55% of the time just to break even.
Model's chance and edge
How likely the model thinks a pick is to win, and how much higher that is than the odds imply. Bigger edge means the model disagrees with DraftKings more.
Result per $100 bet
If you had bet the same amount on every pick, how much you would have won or lost for each $100 bet. A minus sign means a loss.
Combo stats and alt lines
Pts+Reb+Ast adds three stats together. An alt line is an "at least X" bet instead of a simple over/under.
Not betting advice
This is a research tool. The picks have not beaten the sportsbook's fee so far.
